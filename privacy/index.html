Privacy

The app has no accounts and no analytics. Here's every place data goes, including this website.

Last updated 21 October 2026 · Veyndesk 0.5.0

The Veyndesk app has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. There is no Veyndesk company server: the app only talks to the services below, and only to set up a connection.

What is sent, and to whom

ServiceUsed forWhat is sent
ntfy.sh (public message service)Finding your partner by IDA one-time connection offer and answer (network addresses, encryption fingerprint, and the relay address if any) on a topic derived from a hash of the ID.
Google and Cloudflare STUN serversOpening a direct connectionStandard STUN requests, which reveal your public IP address to those servers.
Cloudflare quick tunnel (cloudflared)Relay when a direct connection isn't possibleThe session traffic of relayed sessions only. See encryption.
GitHubDownloading cloudflared on first start (if it isn't already installed)A normal file download.

During a session, the computer you connect to sees your IP address, and you see its computer name.

What is stored on your computer

SystemFolder
Windows%APPDATA%\Veyndesk
macOS~/Library/Application Support/Veyndesk
Linux~/.config/Veyndesk

It holds your ID, a device key, the permanent password hash (if set), your options, the cloudflared program and, if you use the built-in server, its device list and connection log. The viewer remembers your last few partner IDs in the app's local storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Delete the folder to remove everything. Screen content and keystrokes go only to the partner you connected with and are not recorded.

This website

veyndesk.github.io is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (GitHub's privacy statement).

The website (not the app) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, but only if you click Accept in the cookie banner. If you click Reject, or make no choice, Analytics isn't loaded and no Analytics cookies are set. After you accept, it sets cookies and sends Google information such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. See how Google uses this data. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Your choice is saved in your browser only.
