Mac: screen permission

Fix a black screen or non-working mouse when controlling a Mac with Veyndesk: allow Screen Recording and Accessibility in macOS Privacy & Security.

Last updated 21 October 2026 · Veyndesk 0.5.0

macOS doesn't let apps see the screen or control the mouse until you allow it. If your partner sees a black or empty screen, or the mouse and keyboard don't work, do this on the Mac being controlled:

Allow screen recording

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording (on macOS 15 and later: Screen & System Audio Recording). On macOS 12 and earlier: System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Screen Recording.
  2. Turn Veyndesk on. If it isn't listed, click + and choose Veyndesk in Applications.

Allow control (Accessibility)

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  2. Turn Veyndesk on (or add it with +).

Restart Veyndesk

Quit Veyndesk completely (Veyndesk → Quit) and open it again; macOS applies these permissions only after a restart. The partner must reconnect.

Still not working?

  • If you updated Veyndesk, macOS may keep an old entry. Remove Veyndesk from both lists with −, add it again, and restart the app.
  • “Veyndesk can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer”: right-click the app → Open → Open, or use Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. The app isn't notarized yet.

macOS support is new and has not yet been tested by the maintainer on a physical Mac, so please report what you see, including your macOS version and Mac model.
